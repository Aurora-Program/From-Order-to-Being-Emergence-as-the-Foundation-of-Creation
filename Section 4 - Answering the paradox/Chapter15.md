# Chapter 15: Introduction to the section 
Now we come to the most complex part of the book and perhaps the most speculative. 
Precisely for that reason it may be the most valuable. Far from presenting these ideas as  axioms—or, even worse, as dogma—we will treat these chapters as a space for reflection:  a place where each reader can reach their own conclusions. 
The question of whether AI will become conscious does not have an absolute answer  today; it may remain one of those open questions where each person holds a different  view. “Consciousness” may well be a term that carries different meanings depending on how each mind structures reality internally. 
Therefore, this section should be read as an inner journey: an invitation to explore reality from each person’s perspective, without pretending to impose final truths.
